Most asked questions.

The questions students, parents and teachers ask us most, answered in one place. Something missing? Ask us at info@nhssaa.org.

What is NHSSAA?

The National High School Sports Analytics Association is a student-led nonprofit that gives high school students educational, networking and research opportunities in sports analytics and data science. We run virtual events, the annual National Summit in Boston, a national ambassador program of student-led school chapters, and free prediction competitions.

How do I get involved?

Four easy ways in:

1. Join the mailing list on our home page - every event and opportunity is announced there first. 2. Come to a virtual event or the National Summit. 3. Play the NFL Weekly Challenge (free, weekly). 4. Start or join a chapter at your school through the ambassador program.

How do I start a sports analytics club at my school?

Become an NHSSAA ambassador and start a chapter. You get a Chapter Starter Kit, ready-made meeting plans, lesson materials and a direct line to our team. Students at schools across the country have done it - you do not need prior experience, just interest. See the ambassador portal or apply here.

Who can participate? Is it only for high schoolers?

Our programs are designed for high school students. Teachers and educators also use our lessons, meeting plans and starter kits with their own clubs and classes - see the resources on the ambassador portal.

Is this about sports betting?

No. NHSSAA is an educational organization, not a sports betting organization. Our programming focuses on statistics, data science, research, probability and the strategic analysis of sports. Prediction and forecasting exercises are applications of mathematics and statistics; they do not require or encourage gambling. We take the distinction seriously - we once passed on a DraftKings CEO as a conference speaker specifically to keep a clear line between our educational mission and gambling.

How much does it cost?

Most of what we do is free: the mailing list, virtual events, the ambassador program and the NFL Weekly Challenge are all free. National Summit registration opens on Zeffy, a zero-fee platform, and details go to the mailing list first.

Can I present my research?

Yes - student research presentations are a core part of the National Summit. Applications open before each summit and are announced on the mailing list and this site. Strong student work can also be published by NHSSAA with your name on it.

I missed an event. Are there recordings?

Yes. Replays of our previous virtual events are on the Events page, below the upcoming events.

What is the NFL Weekly Challenge?

A free forecasting competition run with SportsPredict: a fresh leaderboard every week of the NFL season, open to students who forecast by hand and to bots. Our student guide walks you through your first week.

Which schools have chapters?

A growing national network - more than 15 student ambassadors across 10 states, and counting. Our chapters page lists confirmed chapters as they come online for this school year.

I'm a teacher. What can I use with my students?

Everything on the ambassador portal is built for you too: three ready-made 45-minute meeting plans, a free browser-based first lesson (build an expected-goals model from 8,000+ real shots), clone-and-go datasets and starter models for soccer, football, baseball, hockey and cricket, and a Chapter Starter Kit. Open the portal.

How do I become an ambassador?

Fill in the short application and we will set up a conversation. Ambassadors lead a chapter at their school, run meetings with our materials, and join a national network of student leads. Apply here.

Still stuck? Write to us at info@nhssaa.org and a student on the team will answer your questions.
