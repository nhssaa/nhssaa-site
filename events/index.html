    NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL 

    SPORTS ANALYTICS SUMMIT 2026

    The NHSSAA National Summit 2026 was held in Boston, MA on March 7, 2026. Over 100 students from around the U.S. participated in discussions with thought-leaders from Major League Baseball, the WNBA, the NBA, various colleges/universities, and tech firms. The students also took part in an active and spirited debate about March Madness, discussed biases in fantasy football decision-making, and participated in a real-time app development exercise.

    WINTER SYMPOSIUM 2026 (VIRTUAL)

    This event took place online on January 24, 2026. Gus Kaeding, the former U.S. Ski & Snowboard Team's Director of Performance Analytics, gave us a great behind-the-scenes view of how the team uses analytics to optimize performance of our Olympians! Then David Bruno & Ramin A. Mohajer from The Alliance for Decision Education led a great talk about making better decisions by learning about biases such as outcome bias, confirmation bias, availability bias, and more!

    FALL SYMPOSIUM 2025 (VIRTUAL)

    This event took place online on November 8, 2025, with featured speakers Thompson Bliss (Senior Manager, Football Operations Data Scientist at the NFL) and sports content creators/influencers Andrew Fenichel (> 300 million lifetime views across all platforms), Frank Michael Smith (>3.5 million followers across socials; also the founder of Rhino Studios), and Cullen Honohan (nearly 2 million followers and 10+ million views/month). The University of Delaware was the presenting sponsor.

    SUMMER SYMPOSIUM 2025 (VIRTUAL)

    This event took place online on June 8, 2025, with featured speakers Meg Rowley (Editor in Chief of FanGraphs), Kiri Oler (Data Scientist, formerly with the Philadelphia Phillies), and Jared Faust (Former Manager of Baseball R&D with the New York Mets and current Director of Basketball Strategy and Analytics at the NBA). YouTube star Zack Hample also joined us to discuss how he has caught over 12,000 baseballs at MLB games.
