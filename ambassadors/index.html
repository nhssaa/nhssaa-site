Start here

  1. Start your chapter. Get the Chapter Starter Kit. It has the pitch, the first email and the setup checklist. Open the starter kit
  2. Run your first meetings. Three ready-made 45-minute meeting plans are in the kit. Pick one, try it, keep what works.
  3. Build and share. Try a lesson, make something small, and share it with NHSSAA. Good student work gets published with your name on it.

What good looks like

  • Your chapter meets about once a month, even for 30 minutes.
  • Everyone tries something hands-on, not just listens.
  • You end each meeting with one next step per person.
  • You tell NHSSAA what happened, so we can help and celebrate it.

Real examples to copy

Three famous sports-analytics ideas, small enough to try in one meeting.

Moneyball

The Oakland A’s won more games by valuing on-base percentage when other teams overlooked it. Try: pick one undervalued stat in your favourite sport and argue for it with five games of evidence.

The shift ban

MLB limited defensive shifts in 2023 after data showed they killed offence. Try: find one rule change in any sport and measure what it changed.

The three-point boom

NBA teams shot far more threes once the math showed they were worth it. Try: chart one team’s shot choices across two seasons. What changed, and why?
