    AMBASSADOR PROGRAM

    The National High School Sports Analytics Association’s mission is to provide high school students with educational, networking, and research opportunities in sports analytics and data science. We are committed to introducing and integrating sports analytics into high schools across the country. Through the new NHSSAA Ambassador Program, which was founded by Amman Malik, we envision a nationwide network of student-led chapters that bring sports analytics nationwide. Together, we will build a national community, spark curiosity, and shape the next generation of sports data leaders.

    ​

    We want to give you the opportunity to start an NHSSAA chapter through a club at your school! We currently have over 8 high schools in the program. The NHSSAA Organizing Committee has launched sports analytics clubs at their high schools. We can help with materials and offer suggestions.  

    ​

    Please contact us at admin@nhssaa.org if you would like to find out more about the Ambassador Program!
